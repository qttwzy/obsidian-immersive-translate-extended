# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project for contributors and users regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, disability, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give credit, protect privacy, and respect licensing boundaries.
- Accept correction and focus on the project's users and maintainability.
- Keep security reports and personal information in the appropriate private channel.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets or private content, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may also issue a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Include links or screenshots only when needed, and remove unrelated personal data. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and conflicts of interest will be avoided when assigning a reviewer.

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone officially represents the project.
